About Electra AI

Built to make democracy understandable for every citizen.

🏛️

Our Mission

Electra AI is an educational platform designed to demystify the democratic process. We believe that an informed voter is the foundation of a healthy democracy. Our interactive tools break down complex electoral procedures into clear, accessible steps — backed by accurate information from the Election Commission of India.

🤖

AI Assistant

Our AI assistant answers your election questions in real-time, adapting to your knowledge level — from beginner to advanced. Ask anything about voter registration, EVMs, model code of conduct, or coalition government formation.

📊

Interactive Learning

Step-by-step process cards, visual timelines, and quizzes make learning engaging and effective. Each section is crafted to build on the previous one for a complete understanding.

🗳️

About Indian Elections

India conducts the world's largest democratic elections. With 970 million+ voters and over 1 million polling stations, the Election Commission of India runs one of the most complex logistical exercises in human history every 5 years.

📱

Mobile Friendly

Fully responsive across all devices. Access your election education on mobile, tablet, or desktop — anywhere, anytime. Built with modern web standards for the best experience.
